In the morning, you cut through the Trojans like a storm. Even the river rises to stop you. It can't. At the gate, Hector waits for you. Alone. Then he sees your face, and he runs. Three times around the walls of Troy. They call you swift-footed for a reason. Finally, he turns to fight. He's wearing your armor. And you know that armor better than anyone. One gap. At the neck. His father watches from the wall. You tie Hector to your chariot, and drag him through the dust. You don't feel better.
