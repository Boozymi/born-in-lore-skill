Then the news reaches you. You pour dirt over your own head. And your mom comes out of the sea. This time, she doesn't fix anything. She just holds your head. Then she tells you the other half of her secret. If you kill Hector, you die right after. Your answer? So be it. You don't even have armor anymore. So you walk to the trench, and you scream. Three times. The Trojans panic. Twelve of their best die in the chaos. That night, the god of blacksmiths makes you new armor. The whole world fits on the shield. Tomorrow, Hector.
