For days, you drag Hector around your friend's grave. Then one night, an old man walks into your tent. Alone. Priam. Troy's king. Hector's father. He kneels, and kisses the hands that killed his son. He asks you to think of your own father. And you both cry. Him for his son. You for your dad, and for Patroclus. You give him back his son, and eleven days of peace to bury him. For eleven days, nobody dies.
