You sail with fifty ships of your own soldiers, the Myrmidons. The whole Greek fleet sets off for Troy, and lands in the wrong country. Long story. When you finally reach Troy, the man waiting for you on the beach is Cycnus, a son of Poseidon. No blade can cut him. So you strangle him with his own helmet strap. Problem solved. Quick pause. Whose life should we live next? Write a name in the comments. The most liked one wins. Then come years of raids. You take city after city. Nothing touches you. Well, almost nothing. They start calling you swift-footed Achilles. Your boss is Agamemnon, king of all the Greeks. He's never at the front, and he always takes the biggest share. He's the king. But you're the best of the Greeks. That's a problem.
