Patroclus can't watch anymore. The Trojans are burning the Greek ships. So he asks for your armor. If the Trojans think you're back, they'll run. You say yes. One rule. Drive them away from the ships. Don't go near the walls. And it works. The Trojans see your armor, and they run. Then he goes near the walls. Apollo knocks the helmet off his head. A spear hits him in the back. And Hector finishes it. Hector takes your armor. Patroclus doesn't come back.
