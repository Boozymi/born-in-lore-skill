Then a seer says Troy can't fall without you. So your mom does what any goddess would do. She hides you. On an island, in a palace full of princesses. As a girl. Your new name is Pyrrha. You're a teenager. It's not going great. Then Odysseus shows up with gifts. Dresses, jewelry, and, for some reason, one shield. Somewhere outside, a war trumpet blows. Every girl in the room runs. One of them grabs a spear. Before you sail, your mom tells you half her secret. Two lives. A long one at home, and nobody ever learns your name. Or a short one, and they sing about you forever. You pick fast. You always do. Congratulations, Pyrrha. You're going to war.
