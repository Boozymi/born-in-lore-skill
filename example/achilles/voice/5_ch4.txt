Without you, the Greeks lose. Badly. They're pushed all the way back to their ships. And you? You sit in your tent and play the lyre. The useless one. You sing about the famous heroes of old. You're not in the songs yet. Then Odysseus shows up. Again. With an offer. Gold, horses, seven cities, a princess, and twenty cauldrons. Just in case. Last time, Odysseus tricked you into this war. This time, you say no. Tomorrow, you sail home.
