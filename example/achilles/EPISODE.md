## Rules for every shot (from `STYLE_BIBLE.md` (skill root), measured)

- One idea per shot; the narration line decides the cut. ~22–28 cuts per minute. Picture never stands still (camera drift,
  breathing, blinks, motes). A cut inside the same set gets its own framing AND its own light, or the cut does not read.
- Text: pop 3–4 frames with overshoot, arrows draw on in ~10 frames after the word, strike 3 frames, joke pause 13 frames,
  stamp 3 frames. Labels sit on the word they name (cue the word, not the sentence).
- Gags get their own backdrop (rays, colour), like chapter I. Low points (V–IX) get no gag devices – only plain name labels.
- Sets: parchment walls with meander, dusk skies, night with moon, silhouettes against bright backlight for big moments.
  The underworld is the only cool grey-lilac palette.
- Our own designs only. Nothing from films, games or the novel. No blood, no gore: deaths happen in silhouette, cut-aways,
  dust and light.
- Characters age on screen: baby → kid (6) → teen (Pyrrha) → adult. No old Achilles.
