# LIFE PLAN – "What If You Were Born as Achilles?"

Our own episode in the Born-in-Lore format, planned with the skill's `STYLE_BIBLE.md` and the template in
`SCRIPT_FORMULA.md` §8.

**Why Achilles:** his story is almost 3,000 years old and belongs to everyone (public domain). Everybody knows the
name and the heel, nobody owns the character. He is Greek and a warrior, so he sits perfectly next to Kratos, the
biggest video of the channel.
**Rights rule:** our own designs only. Nothing from the film *Troy* (2004), the games *Hades* or *Assassin's Creed
Odyssey*, or the novel *The Song of Achilles*. Only the ancient sources listed at the bottom.

**Script and shots:** every line of narration and every shot is in `SCRIPT.md`; this file is the plan for the whole
life, chapter by chapter.

## 2. Hook (0:00–0:15 = 38 words, then the baby sentence of 24 words opens the prologue)

> If you were born as Achilles, you'd kill lions at six, then a son of Poseidon, then Troy's greatest prince, in front of his own father. And you'd pick that life on purpose, knowing exactly how short it is.
> But right now, you're a baby, hanging upside down by one heel over the river of the dead, and you're already screaming for attention.

- Formula check: 3 escalating deeds (lions → a god's son → the greatest prince) + turn sentence (the decision, like Kratos' "very bad deal", loops into the final question) + baby sentence with symbol and core trait.
- Picture: lightning-flash open like Kratos is *their* device – ours opens on the black river: the baby swings into frame upside down, name tag "ACHILLES (that's you)" pops in 3 frames.
