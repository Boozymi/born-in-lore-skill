# LIFE PLAN – "What If You Were Born as ___?"

Length: no fixed target – the story decides (Born in Lore: 6:52–14:17, the newest 13–14 min) · words ≈ seconds × 2.6 ·
prologue + chapters I–IX · 12–18 s end card · every part as densely cut as the prologue.
An original or public-domain character: the deeds in the hook must make sense without knowing the character.

## The blanks
| field | yours |
|---|---|
| NAME | |
| CORE TRAIT (one word) | angry / wants to win / wants to be remembered … |
| SYMBOL (an object, 4×) | baby sentence → twice in the middle → "Not bad for …" |
| THE DECISION | made in chapter I, becomes the final question |
| PLANT 1 – a secret | → payoff at 50–75 % |
| PLANT 2 – an object of a parent | → payoff |
| PLANT 3 – a name or a lesson | → payoff |
| "THIS TIME" (exactly 2×) | the same situation, the outcome flipped |
| A TITLE THAT TURNS | a nickname from chapter I/II that means something new at the end |

## Hook (0:00–0:13, 30–38 words)
If you were born as [NAME], you'd [deed 1], then [bigger deed], then [biggest deed].
And [turn: origin "it all starts with …" | betrayal | death | mercy].
But right now, you're a baby [place], and you're already [CORE TRAIT].

## Prologue (to ~1:55, ~250 words, short sentences, ~3.5 jokes per minute)
parents one line each (plant 1) · sibling/friend with one quirk (plant 2) · "At [age], …" a childhood scene that shows the trait ·
first loss · a one-line time jump · adult status quo · the people you will lose (plant 3)

## Chapters I–IX (as long as the moment needs: 30–60 s in an 8-minute episode, up to ~3 min in a 14-minute one; title 1–4 words)
Start with "Then …", an age, a place or the next action – never a recap. One iconic moment per chapter. End on a 2–6 word button.
I the decision · II rise + comment question at ~38 % ("Quick pause. Who should be born next? Most-liked comment wins.") ·
III–IV twists/payoffs · V biggest set piece → low point, no jokes · VI new world · VII journey · VIII final fight, letting go ·
IX meet the past, the title turns, a quiet last image

## Ending (last 12 s)
Not bad for a [image from the baby sentence].
You [greatest deed], [greatest loss], and still [human choice].      ← past tense
So, tell me, would you still [THE DECISION]?

## Voice check
second person · present tense · "you'd" only in the hook · past tense only after "Not bad for" · ~9 words per sentence,
≥ 30 % of sentences ≤ 5 words · "This time" exactly twice · ~2 jokes per minute, none at the low points or in the last minute
